## NEW CANDIDATES

**One candidate cleared the bar this week: AXT Inc. (`AXTI`).**

**Chain:** AI boom (CONSENSUS, root) → Optical interconnect & co-packaged optics (CONSENSUS) → CPO supply chain: lasers, fiber attach, photonic foundry (FRONTIER) → **AXT Inc. — InP wafer substrate supply.**

**Claim:** Nvidia's $2B-each equity lock-ups with Coherent and Lumentum (announced 2026-03-02) pushed the real bottleneck one layer further upstream, to the indium-phosphide wafer substrates both companies' laser lines depend on — and AXT has now signed two independent, dated, SEC-filed multi-year supply agreements confirming it is that upstream chokepoint for both.

**Evidence:**
- AXT signed a Master Development and Supply Agreement with Coherent effective **2026-06-25** (through June 2029) for 6-inch InP wafer substrates: Coherent prepays $22.3M against future shipments, AXT commits to expanding its Beijing facility 2026–2028, and Coherent gets first right to capacity built above its committed volume at the same pricing ([AXT SEC 8-K, exhibit ex_993544](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001051627/000143774926024883/ex_993544.htm), filed 2026).
- AXT signed a separate definitive supply agreement with Lumentum, announced **2026-07-29**: a minimum annual InP commitment through **2031**, with Lumentum paying two $43.5M deposits (~$87M total) applied as shipment credits ([Globe and Mail/Business Wire](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/AXTI-Q/pressreleases/3563054/axt-secures-long-term-indium-phosphide-supply-agreement/), 2026-07-29; also filed as SEC 8-K per [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/AXTI/8-k-axt-inc-reports-material-event-4d7b24b54c4d.html)).
- AXT shares rose ~17–19% on the Coherent news alone ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/axt-signs-major-inp-wafer-supply-agreement), 2026); the two independent agreements corroborate each other rather than resting on a single source.

**Honesty flags, stated plainly:**
- AXT has already re-rated sharply on both prints — this is not a stealth entry at a bargain price. Optics broadly is one of the most crowded AI-infra trades already (Coherent +28% YTD, Lumentum +71% YTD as of late July, [247wallst.com](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/31/applied-optoelectronics-and-coherent-gain-7-lumentum-adds-5-as-risk-on-feeling-spreads-to-optics-stocks/), 2026-07-31), and this thesis rides the same wave one layer upstream.
- Lumentum's own stock slipped on its AXT deal — analysts read the $87M prepaid-deposit commitment as a sign of capacity/flexibility strain on Lumentum's side, a nuance that cuts against reading this purely as bullish news for the ecosystem ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/catalyst/lumentum-stock-slips-as-costly-supply-deal-raises-doubts), 2026).
- **AXTI could not be added to the scoreboard this session** — `scripts/scoreboard.py add` requires a live price fetch with no cache fallback for a brand-new ticker, and live Yahoo access is blocked this session (the same structural gap that has stalled `PWR` for three weeks running). See DIG DEEPER.

**Dies if:** CPO switch architectures fully integrate light sources within the package (eliminating discrete external lasers, and the InP wafer demand behind them, as a distinct category) before ~2028, or a second qualified InP-wafer supplier reaches volume parity with AXT, removing the single-chokepoint framing.
