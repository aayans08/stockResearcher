### Sunrise Energy Metals (ASX: `SRL`; US OTCQX: `SREMF`)

**Chain:** AI boom (CONSENSUS, root) → Data center buildout (CONSENSUS) → Power generation & grid interconnection (CURRENT) → On-site power: fuel cells & bridge generation (CURRENT) → Scandium supply for solid-oxide fuel cells (**CURRENT**, promoted this week) → **SRL — the first US-government-backed Western primary-scandium supply response.**

**Claim:** If Bloom Energy's SOFC deployment scales as its backlog implies, scandium oxide becomes the binding constraint before fuel-cell manufacturing capacity does; SRL's Syerston project would be the world's first *primary* scandium mine (vs. today's byproduct-only global supply), directly addressing the China-concentration risk this tree has tracked since July.

**Evidence:**
- US Dept of War's Office of Strategic Capital signed a conditional up-to-$400M, 25-year debt commitment for Syerston, targeting ~60 t/yr of high-purity scandium oxide over a ~32-year mine life, with DoW getting right-of-first-offer on output ([US Dept of War](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4566598/office-of-strategic-capital-signs-400-million-conditional-loan-commitment-with/), 2026-08-08).
- Coverage crossed into US financial mainstream: CNBC video interview with Sunrise's CEO ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/08/11/sunrise-energy-metals-ceo-on-u-s-defense-deal-we-know-the-risk-of-relying-on-china.html), 2026-08-11); Bloomberg covered the +16–29% stock move ([Bloomberg via Kalkine](https://kalkine.com.au/news/mining/sunrise-energy-metals-asxsrl-jumps-as-pentagon-backs-scandium-project), 2026-08-10).
- SRL trades OTCQX in the US as `SREMF` ([OTC Markets](https://www.otcmarkets.com/stock/SREMF/quote), 2026) — accessible without a foreign brokerage, but OTCQX liquidity/spreads are materially worse than the ASX primary listing; no NYSE/Nasdaq ADR exists.

**Honesty flags, stated plainly:**
- The $400M facility is a **conditional term sheet**, not a closed loan — the primary source explicitly states "no assurance that definitive financing documents will be executed or that financial close will occur."
- This is a single-project, small-cap story (~AUD 3B market cap) already up sharply on the news — the least de-risked entry point of any candidate this memo has surfaced, and OTC-only US access is a real liquidity caveat, not a footnote.
- The underlying Bloom-vs-short-seller scandium-shortage dispute this node also tracks remains unresolved either way — SRL's promotion is about the *supply-response* side of that dispute, not a resolution of it.

**Dies if:** the conditional DoW financing fails to close, or Syerston's feasibility/permitting timeline slips materially against its stated schedule, removing the concrete Western-supply catalyst that justified this candidate.

**Theme tags:** `ai-infrastructure`, `materials`.

### Almonty Industries (Nasdaq: `ALM`)

**Chain:** AI boom (CONSENSUS, root) → Chips/GPUs & accelerators (CONSENSUS) → advanced-node/HBM fab scaling (CONSENSUS context) → WF6 / tungsten supply chain (**FRONTIER**, new node this week) → **ALM — positioning to become the largest tungsten producer outside China.**

**Claim:** Japanese WF6 producers permanently ceasing production after China cut off tungsten-powder feedstock pushes the AI chip-fab supply chain's scarcity down to raw tungsten ore itself; Almonty's Sangdong mine (South Korea, ramping toward ~460,000 MTU/yr by 2027) plus its existing Panasqueira (Portugal) operation position it as the clearest non-Chinese ore-layer beneficiary.

**Honesty flags, stated plainly:**
